HOW IT WORKS

Higher yield isn't always better yield

The top of any yield dashboard is usually token-reward farms, rates that spiked last week, and pools too small to exit. StableRoute filters them out before it picks.

Live yields, six checks

Pulls every stablecoin pool from DeFiLlama, then checks pool size, your share of it, real versus token-reward yield, sudden spikes, track record and outlook.

You sign, it never holds funds

For Aave and Compound picks it drafts the deposit: approve exactly your amount, then supply. You review and sign both in your own wallet.

A pick you can check

Every answer comes with a Groth16 proof, verified on zkVerify, that the pick passed every check and beat every other pool that passed.
